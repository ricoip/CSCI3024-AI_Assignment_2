## Scope and attribution

Implemented: paper Eqs. 5-10 and 19-28 (supervised classification and uncertainty).
Adapted: small 64-128-10 MLP instead of ResNet; softplus + 1 rather than
exponential concentration; local 8x8 digits instead of Office-Home/VisDA.
Omitted: unsupervised uncertainty guidance, active query selection, certainty
pseudo-labeling, and the full active domain adaptation benchmark.

This demonstrates the core probabilistic method. It does not reproduce the
paper's full pipeline or its reported benchmark performance. Uncertainty is
conditional on the learned Dirichlet model and is not a guarantee of correctness.
